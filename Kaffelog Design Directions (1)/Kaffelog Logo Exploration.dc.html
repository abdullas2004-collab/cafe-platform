DIRECTION 02 · OPERATIONS DESK — MARK EXPLORATION
THE KAFFELOG MARK
Five routes, all built from the same two ideas: the logged tick and the milk-level oval. Each is drawn on the 8-unit grid the product already uses, so the mark and the UI share one geometry.
REF · KFL-02-MARK
ROUTES A–E
FOR REVIEW
ROUTE A · THE STAMP
A hard square containing the milk oval and a single copper base rule — reads as an inspection stamp at any size.
BEST FOR · APP ICON, STAMPS, PDF SEALS
RISK · CAN FEEL BUREAUCRATIC
ROUTE B · MILK LEVEL
A carton silhouette whose fill is the data: the level shifts to show today's stock. Literal, but instantly café.
BEST FOR · PRODUCT, LOADING STATES
RISK · TOO SINGLE-PURPOSE FOR A BRAND
ROUTE C · THE LOGGED TICK ★
The oval plus one signal cell in the corner — the cell recolours to carry SAFE / DUE SOON / NEEDS ATTENTION. The mark is also the status system.
BEST FOR · EVERYTHING; SCALES TO 16PX
RISK · NEEDS DISCIPLINED COLOUR USE
ROUTE D · THE LEDGER K
A K whose arms are two log rows meeting the stem at mid-height — the shape of a signed-off sheet. Purely typographic, no imagery.
BEST FOR · WORDMARK LOCKUPS, MERCH
RISK · LESS OWNABLE AS A LONE ICON
ROUTE E · PERFORATION
The receipt tear-line becomes the container. Softest, most hospitality-leaning of the five.
BEST FOR · PACKAGING, PRINT, SOCIAL
